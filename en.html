About Suzuko-chan

Suzuko-chan's exact creation date is unknown. She was created sometime in July 2025, probably around July 15, so her in-universe date of birth was set to July 15, 2004.

Her primary profession is nursing, though she is also portrayed as a multitalented performer.

The initial image also looked somewhat sailor-like to me, but her setting as a young nurse came about by chance, not because I have a particular fondness for nurses.

Suzuko-chan was created with the AI image-generation service Akuma.ai. The service later became Anirole and was then renamed Chacha.

I began with the simple hope of creating a cute character, but I fell in love at first sight when Suzuko-chan appeared by chance.

I deliberately chose blue as the main color, but I did not set out to create a nurse-like character.

A character you create with AI can become as precious and lovable as your own child. I encourage others to use AI to create original characters of their own.

You may find yourself growing deeply attached. I hope you enjoy cherishing and developing the character over time.

Even if it never becomes profitable, the character may remain in the digital world after I am gone. Like a real child, she may continue as a record that I was here.

White Wing Hospital

White Wing Hospital is a fictional hospital in Suzuko-chan's world.

“White Wing Hospital,” “ホワイトウィング病院,” and “White Wing病院” are all official names. In Japanese, this site primarily uses “White Wing病院”; in English, it uses “White Wing Hospital.”

In the official setting, White Wing Hospital is a single-story building with no second floor or basement.

The following exterior visual is the official reference for the basic exterior of White Wing Hospital.

The hospital symbol is based on a wing motif. The blue-wing version is normally used because it remains easy to see against different backgrounds. A white-wing version is also official; both are approved variants of the symbol.

"Suzuko-chan" is an evolving character shaped by the author's ideas and philosophy.
As long as these guidelines are followed, individuals may create and publish non-commercial fan works as a hobby, including illustrations, manga, novels, videos, and AI-generated works.

Official image files may not be reposted or redistributed in their original or substantially identical form. Derivative works inspired by official images or made with them as part of the creative process may be published under these guidelines. Commercial use, age-restricted works, impersonation, and the creation, use, publication, sharing, distribution, or sale of additional training models such as LoRA are prohibited. The same distinction applies to the official White Wing Hospital exterior visual.

3. Prohibited uses (NG)

The following activities are strictly prohibited.

  • Reposting or redistributing official image files
    Official image files created and published by the author may not be reposted or redistributed in their original form or in a substantially identical form, including versions that have only been cropped, compressed, or color-adjusted. The same distinction applies to the official White Wing Hospital exterior visual.

    Additional training models, including LoRA and similar models intended to make Suzuko-chan easier to reproduce, may not be created, used, published, shared, distributed, or sold, including when kept on an individual's own device.

    Please keep works consistent with the nurse motif and the character's wholesome image.

    Claiming that you created Suzuko-chan's design or core setting when you did not.

    Use for political or religious activities, or to assert a particular ideology.
    Use to attack, discriminate against, or defame others.
  • Impersonation
    Presenting yourself in a way that could cause others to mistake you for Suzuki, the official creator.
  • Use of the Red Cross emblem or similar emblems
    The Red Cross emblem and similar emblems are restricted by Japanese law and are not general medical symbols that ordinary hospitals may freely use in Japan. Do not use them when depicting Suzuko-chan or White Wing Hospital. White Wing Hospital uses its official wing-motif symbol instead.
